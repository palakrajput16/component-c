## Simple Definitions

- **Mean AUC:** Average AUC across the 5 folds.
- **Winner:** Classifier with the highest mean AUC.
- **Kendall Tau:** Measures how similar the observed ranking is to the true ranking.
- **Optimism:** Observed winner AUC − true AUC of that winner.
- **p-value:** Result of the paired t-test comparing c1 and c2.
- **Correct detection:** p < 0.05 and c1 wins.
- **Sign error:** p < 0.05 and c2 wins.
- **No detection:** p ≥ 0.05.
